# GRAFT

**On your phone:** open the artifact link. The whole game is one page and this PC does not
need to be on.

**On this PC:** double-click `Play.bat` (opens here, prints a QR for your phone).
`Test.bat` runs the 22 checks.

A third-person astronaut on a small planet with an ocean. **Everything you can see, you can
make** — the world is full of trees, blooms, spires and kelp, and every one of them is
something you can plant yourself. Plant, walk away, come back to something bigger.

- `CLAUDE.md` — what is built, what is measured, and the landmine list. Read this first.
- `tools/tune_terrain.mjs` — re-runs the buildable-land sweep.

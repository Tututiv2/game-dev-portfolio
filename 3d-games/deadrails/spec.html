Design spec (Dead Rails–style HTML build)

Play prototype

Scope — Fan design document to inspire an original browser game inspired by Dead Rails (RCM Games, Roblox). This page mirrors the project plan: systems, win condition, and technical mapping. The playable slice lives in index.html with config.json for tuning.

Part 1 — High concept and win condition

  • Weird West / late-1800s desert rail run through undead and outlaws.
  • Crew defends a steam train and scavenges between safe outposts.
  • Run length ~80 km; outposts often spaced ~10 km; finale at the bridge in this prototype.
  • Core tension: fuel + ammo scarcity vs defense and forays off-train to loot.

Part 2 — The train

  • Furnace: coal, corpses, paper as fuel. Empty tank stops the train; stopped trains take integrity pressure.
  • Barricades (newspaper/scrap) reduce night pressure; “weld” action mapped to a key.
  • Forward travel consumes fuel; speed and burn are data-driven in config.json.

Part 3 — Player / party

  • Health, bandages, ~10 stack slots, melee + revolver in prototype.
  • Bonds in meta: earned on a full run, stored in localStorage.

Part 4 — Economy

  • Cash for buys; sell gold, corpses, scrap at the outpost counter.
  • Outpost restocks coal and ammo per outpostShops in config.

Part 5 — Combat / equipment

  • Revolver + ammo; shooting stabilizes train integrity in this prototype (abstract “holding the line”).
  • Elite or rare weapons can be added later in loot tables.

Part 6 — Enemies, day/night

  • Day/night from in-game hours; four moon “flavors” rotate by distance band.
  • Night procs extra pressure on player HP and train integrity, mitigated by barricades.

Part 7 — World / POI

  • Linear distance drives outpost triggers. Finale at configured finaleAtMeters.
  • More POIs (bank, castle) can be added as staged events in data.

Part 8 — UI/UX

  • Canvas view + HUD: fuel, health, train integrity, distance, outpost target, time.
  • Modal for outpost trading and bridge finale; event log in side panel.

Part 9 — Technical (browser)

  • Plain Canvas 2D + IIFE; requestAnimationFrame game loop; config merge on load.
  • Splitting into js/modules is possible when the project grows.

Part 10 — File layout

  • deadrails-html/index.html — game shell
  • css/main.css — layout and HUD
  • js/game.js — logic and drawing
  • config.json — balance tuning
  • assets/ — optional sprites/audio

Tree spawning

0.25

Target tree count is the planet-wide scenery budget — all biomes share it, each one taking its proportional share based on density. Clustering controls scatter style: 0 = perfectly even spread (every tree equidistant), 1 = tight clumps of trees around a few hotspots (dense forests with open clearings between). ~0.25 looks natural for most biomes.

Biomes (latitude bands)

Latitude is degrees from −90 (south pole) to +90 (north pole). Bands can overlap — the narrowest band covering a latitude wins, so a small "beach" band at ±6°–18° correctly takes priority over a wide "forest" band at ±65°. Density is the per-slot spawn probability (0 = empty, 1 = packed). Presets are the LT2 tree presets eligible to spawn in that band — pick multiple for variety (oak + cherry + default in a forest), or just one (palm on beaches).

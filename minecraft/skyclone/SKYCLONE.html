Play it

  1. Double-click Dev Server.bat in the SkyblockClone folder (or ask Claude to start it).
  2. In Minecraft 26.2 (26.1.x and 26.3 also work, via ViaVersion): Multiplayer → Direct Connect → localhost:25630.
  3. You land on your private island. The sidebar Objective walks you through the early game; the Nether Star in slot 9 is the SkyBlock Menu for everything else.
  4. Admin tools (once op'd): /sb help, /sb give <player> <item>, /sb selftest, /commands lists every command.

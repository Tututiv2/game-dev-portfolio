## Kept on purpose (owner may want to review)

Material coinages that read as ordinary nouns of a fantasy world rather than as a named character,
boss or place were left alone: Mithril, Titanium, Tungsten, Umber, Glacite, the gemstone types
(Ruby, Jade, Amber, Topaz...), Hemovibe, Hemoglass, Vinesap, Caducous, Agaricus, Berberis, Timite,
Youngite, Obsolite, Highlite, Treasurite, Starfall, Sorrow, Ectoplasm, Volta, Magmag, Helixis,
Figstone, Mangcore, Living Metal, Null Sphere/Ovoid, Derelict Ashe. Renaming any of them is one line in
`RENAME_WHOLE` / `RENAME_TOKENS` of the generator; ids never change.

# foraging — renames

Internal ids keep the reference game's id; only what players see changed. Generic words ("Birch Park",
"Spruce Woods", "Dark Thicket", "Savanna Woodland", "Jungle Island", "Howling Cave", "Sweep", "Foraging
Fortune", "Tree Gift", "Heart of the Forest", "Forest Whispers", "Rookie/Promising/Sweet/Efficient/Decent
Axe") are kept. Fig and Mangrove are real trees and stay; "Helix" follows the collections/materials
modules' rename to **Spiral**, "Moonglade"/"Torrhus" follow the accessories module's **Moonmarsh**/**Torrent**.

# foraging — hand-off

Package `com.skyclone.mod.foraging` (entry `ForagingModule`), generators in `.gen` (`ParkGenerator`,
`MarshGenerator`, helpers `GenKit`, `MarshFlora`), data in `resources/data/foraging/` generated by
`py -3 tools/gen/foraging/gen_foraging.py` (items, recipes, mobs, spawns, NPC skins — never hand-edit).
Member data key **`foraging`** (`ForagingData`).

## What works (verified how)
Verified with `bash tools/check.sh foraging` -> **CHECK OK**, **8 JUnit tests pass**
(`plugin/src/test/java/com/skyclone/mod/foraging/ForagingPureTest.java`: Sweep/Fortune/Tree Gift/HotF formulas
against research 07, the wood table, and BOTH islands generated + flood-checked: zero unreachable anchors,
nothing written out of bounds), and `tools/preview.sh` on both islands (PNGs read and judged, `RESULT: OK`).
**Not run live** (agents don't start the server) — the 6 self-tests and the `/sb foraging` tools are for that.

- **Chopping** (`Chopper`, a BreakHandler, priority 400) on **The Park**, the **Moonmarsh** and the **Hub's
  `forest` area** (dial `foraging.hub-forest-only`). One swing fells `1 + Sweep` logs (cap 36) of the tree you hit,
  breadth-first from the hit log, in a quick wave; tough Moonmarsh woods use `1 + min(20, Sweep / (1 + t/10))`
  (fig 10 / mangrove 50 / spiral 150, research 07 §4.3-4.4). Drops = logs x (1 + Foraging Fortune/100) through
  `Sky.loot().give(.., FORAGING)` (collections + sacks see them), Foraging XP per log (6 / 15 / 20 / 25) via
  `SkillService`, one `SkyEvents.Gather(FORAGING, "<BLOCK>_LOG", drops)` per swing. A log only counts if its
  connected log body touches leaves (dial), so log-framed buildings are never choppable; on our islands every
  building uses planks / stripped logs anyway. Flowers in those zones are picked (1 XP) and regrow.
- **Regrowth** (`TreeRegen`): the first cut records the WHOLE tree; it regrows at once after 60 s (dial), never
  half-cut, postponed while a player stands inside it. Pending trees are saved to `plugins/SkyClone/foraging-regen.json`
  off-thread every 30 s and restored at the next boot (crash safety); restored synchronously on disable.
- **Private island**: natural logs (BUILD zone, vanilla break) also give Foraging XP, fortune extras and `Gather`;
  logs the player placed are remembered in the chunk PDC and give nothing (no place-break farm). Vanilla axe
  log-stripping is blocked on the Park / Moonmarsh / Hub so trees can't be ruined.
- **Axes** (19 items incl. the 5 vanilla axes with proper damage, `vanilla` tag): Rookie (gains Efficiency I on
  first fell), Promising (Efficiency I after 50 logs, counter in lore), Sweet (20% Apple per log), Efficient
  (+5 planks per log), **Sprucecutter / Timberfeller** (+4 / +25 Sweep, **Throwing Axe** right-click: flies up to
  24 blocks as a spinning ItemDisplay and fells at 50% Sweep, 1 s cooldown; +50 / +100 Foraging Fortune in the
  Park), Sculptor's Axe (carves logs into 6-sided wood on your own island), Seriously Damaged / Decent axes,
  **Fig Hatchet / Figstone Cleaver / Spiral Chopper** with Frenzy (+1 Sweep per 2k/10k/20k Moonmarsh logs,
  stored on the axe, shown in lore, fed to stats through an ItemStatContributor). 7 recipes from NEU with
  collection unlocks (Timberfeller needs Jungle Log VII). Two travel scrolls (Jungle Island, Howling Cave)
  unlock `/warp jungle` / `/warp howl` when read.
- **The Park** (`park`, ~480x350): five floating forests climbing west, joined by plank bridges — Birch Park
  (spawn, portal to the Hub, fountain plaza, Axe-trader Hewitt's stall, Ranger Ellery's lodge, ponds), Spruce
  Woods (snow, pines, Kestrel's cabin, frozen pond, the arch down into the **Howling Cave** — a tunnel to a

  Savanna Woodland (terraced mesa, **Lyra's Plateau** with a harp ring, the **Ember Trials** fire pit) and
  Jungle Island (jungle giants with cocoa and vines, Mossbeard's hut, the Norse Camp longhouse, the shaman's hut,
  the jungle warp clearing). Zones per forest gated Foraging I..V, SAFE spawn plaza, 21 fairy souls (boulder
  tops, cave nooks, behind buildings), lamps along every route. Anchors for the shops module's
  `npc.merry_norseman`, `npc.strategist`, `npc.shaman` are there.
- **Howling Cave mobs**: Pack Wraith (Lv30, 6,000 HP), Howling Wraith (Lv35, 7,000), Shade of the Alpha (Lv55,
  31,150, bites for TRUE damage, one at a time in the den). Stats/drops from research 03 §7.3 + NEU, slayer
  `wolf`, bestiary families `pack_spirit` / `howling_spirit` / `soul_of_the_alpha`, `extra.types animal,wolf`.
- **Park NPCs**: five keepers' 3-stage log requests (64/256/1,024 of their forest's wood, coins + Foraging XP;
  Ranger's last gives a Sprucecutter, Kestrel's a Howling Cave scroll, Mossbeard's a Jungle Island scroll);
  **Lyra's harp** (8 songs: watch the strings light up, play them back; first clean play = permanent
  Intelligence, 18 total); **Ember Trials** (feed logs from every forest + your last badge to earn
  Campfire Badge I..XXIX, the accessories module's `CAMPFIRE_TALISMAN_n`); **Axe-trader Hewitt** (buy the

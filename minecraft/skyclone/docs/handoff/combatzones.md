## Known risks
1. **The dragon in a NORMAL world** (we chose NORMAL + fixed midnight over THE_END to avoid the vanilla exit-portal
   podium being built in the middle of the island): its vanilla AI pathed around the world ORIGIN and froze (playtest-endgame), so it is now held in HOVER and flown by `DragonFlight` (see the playtest-endgame section below).
   Melee on dragon parts relies on Paper firing `EntityDamageByEntityEvent` on the part (we route it) or on the
   dragon (core routes it) — one of the two must hold.
2. **Mined blocks regrow on a timer held in memory**; a crash mid-regrow leaves bedrock in the quarry/gravel pit
   (a clean shutdown restores them). `/sb island build <id> force` repairs it.
3. **Zone requirements are baked into the End layout** at build time (layouts persist); changing the hideout/tomb
   dials needs a version bump or `/sb island build the_end force`. Warp requirements read the dials at boot.

## Playtest 2026-10-03 (playtest-loops) — Spider's Den pacing: FIXED (hunting grounds)
Finding: "[major] 25 low-tier spiders spread over 12 anchors. After 9 kills nothing respawned within 40 blocks for
~4 minutes, and many spiders sit on terrain the player cannot reach. Spider T1 took ~8 min for 102/250 Combat XP."

**Root cause.** Core's spawner cycles a rule's anchors ROUND-ROBIN and does not look at where the player is. The
generator gave each band ONE island-wide anchor list (`spawn.mob.low.*` = 46 points on a ring ~560 blocks round;
mid 34, high 28), and 8 low rules (25 mobs) all drew from it. A kill in front of you respawned at the next anchor in
the cycle — anywhere on the mountain — so the pack in front of you only ever drained. Leash is 24 blocks from the
NEAREST anchor of the rule, and with anchors everywhere that is no leash at all, so spiders climbed onto the terraced
north/east faces, rock spires and the forest canopy. `walk.ignore` skipped every `spawn.mob.` anchor, so nothing
checked reachability (2 mid anchors were in fact unreachable).

**Fix (generator v1 -> v2, spawns.json, all inside combatzones).**
- `gen/HuntingGrounds.java` (new, pure): scores every 3-block lattice point of a band on the BUILT canvas — columns
  with a <=1-block slope, head room and no road/structure count for, cliff columns (slope >=3) count -3 — within 14
  blocks of a road; greedily picks N grounds (first within 70 of the Den Entrance, the rest >=64/48/40 apart),
  relaxing thresholds rather than ever returning fewer than the rules name. 12 anchors per ground inside
  `RADIUS` 13, centre-out, 4 apart.
- `DenGenerator`: grounds picked after roads (step 7b), BEFORE greenery: trees (trunk margin + canopy), boulders and
  webs are kept out of `CLEAR` (19) around every ground; spires are avoided. Anchors are `spawn.mob.<band>.<g>.<n>`
  (low a-d, mid a-c, high a-b) with layout data `ground.<band>.<g>` = `x,z,r,score,cliffs`. **Every** mob anchor (also
  nest/gravel/slime) is now filtered through the same Walkability flood preview uses (run in-generator on the
  MemoryCanvas, ~130 ms) and `walk.ignore` is gone, so preview / `combatzones.walkable` now enforce it.
  Version bumped to 2 (rebuilds the Den; also picks up the `Builders.well` ladder fix for Granny's well).
- `spawns.json` (via `tools/gen/combatzones/gen_combatzones.py`; items/mobs/zealot outputs regenerate byte-identical):
  one rule set per ground, `activationRange` 48. Low ground = 10 spiders (2+2 Splitter Lv2/4, 2 Dasher Lv4, 1 Dasher
  Lv6, 1+1 Weaver Lv3/5, 1 Jockey Lv3), respawn 10-15 s, plus a Voracious Lv10 in b and d (41 low spiders total, was
  25). Mid ground 7 (+1 Voracious in a), high ground 6, respawn 12-20 s. Nest/gravel/burrow/slime/End unchanged.
- Shipping seed: low a (-37,77) 46 blocks from spawn, b (-88,32), c (-88,-37), d (59,-82) — all 444-508 of 531
  columns open, 0 cliff columns. mid a (38,41) b (-46,35) c (59,-16, 18 cliff cols); high a (35,-40) b (41,11).

**Verified.** `bash tools/check.sh combatzones` CHECK OK. `tools/preview.sh ...DenGenerator` RESULT OK with no
ignore (walk.png: nine tight clusters, all green). New unit test `DenHuntingGroundsTest` (5) + `DragonMathTest` (6):
**11/11 pass** — every spawn anchor REACHABLE and none skipped; every ground >=8 anchors inside its radius; low.a

packs >=6 (low >=10), respawn <=25 s (the old `spawn.mob.low.*` rules fail it). Self-test `combatzones.anchors`

## Playtest 2026-10-03 (playtest-endgame) — frozen dragon: FIXED (own flight); eye mis-click + sidebar colours: FIXED
Findings: "[major] After 8 eyes the Protector Dragon froze at (-48,104,-12), ~110 blocks from the nest, in vanilla
DragonPhase 2 (landing approach), for minutes; its Rush still hit anyone within 110 blocks of the podium for 550 true
damage; melee impossible, admin kill needed." · "[minor] Right-clicking a filled frame while holding a Summoning Eye
takes your eye back; 'Eyes placed' and the dragon sidebar line are dark purple / dark gray."

**Root cause.** The vanilla dragon AI plans every phase over a ring of path nodes built around the world ORIGIN
(and `setPodium` does not move that ring). The nest is at (52, -42), so the dragon circled the origin, and the
landing approach found no path from there and parked for good. Separately, Rush was an instant hit on any player within
a fixed 110 blocks of the podium, unrelated to where the body was — so a parked dragon kept killing people.

**Fix.**
- `DragonFlight.java` (new, pure): our own flight model. CIRCLE (orbit r=38, +32 over the podium, 10-17 s, the
  crystal towers stand at r=26 with tops <= +27) -> SWOOP (level at +32 until inside r=19, then dives) -> PERCH (6 s
  hovering +2.5 over the podium top = in sword reach of the dais) -> CIRCLE (climbs nearly straight up out of the ring).
  Steering is a smoothed velocity toward a moving aim point, capped at `BASE_SPEED 0.75 b/t x variant speed` (clamped
  0.6..1.8, so Young/Old speeds are real now). Rush = CHARGE: it flies at the player (homing, x1.6 speed, 6 s max,
  target leashed to r=60) and only contact (<= 6 blocks) deals the damage + knockback.
- `DragonFight`: the dragon is put in vanilla **HOVER** (verified in the 26.1.2 server bytecode: `aiStep` skips all
  movement when the phase is HOVERING, but still updates the hit-box parts, which NoAI would not) and teleported every
  tick to the model; `EnderDragonChangePhaseEvent` cancels any phase other than HOVER/DYING for our dragon. A
  **no-movement watchdog** runs every second: body not moving for 5 s outside a perch, or > 12 blocks from the model
  -> log a warning, re-assert HOVER, continue the model from where the body is; three trips in a row -> teleport to
  `boss.dragon` above the nest. Crystal beams follow every 5 ticks.
- Abilities only target players inside the new dial **`combatzones.dragon-arena-radius`** (64, was a fixed 110; spectators
  excluded). Rush only starts from CIRCLE, so it never cuts a perch (the melee window) short; Rush's message now warns
  ("is rushing at you!") and the hit message arrives on contact.
- Altar: a Summoning Eye on a filled frame never takes anything back ("This frame already holds your eye. Place it in
  an empty frame"); taking an eye back needs the **Sleeping Eye in hand** (what its lore and this hand-off always said).
- Readability: `DragonMath.readable(char)` maps dark colour codes to their light twin (8->7, 5->d, ...). Used on the
  sidebar, title, chat and menus; variant identity colours are unchanged. Sidebar: `&fEyes placed: &d3&f/&d8`,
  `&fDragon: &dRising...`.

**Verified.** `bash tools/check.sh combatzones` CHECK OK. Unit tests (JUnit launcher, outside gradle):
`DragonFlightTest` 8 + `DragonMathTest` 7 = **15/15** — 20 seeds x 3 speeds x 5 min: never > r46 from the podium,
never still for 1 s outside a perch; >= 5 perches and >= 500 in-reach ticks per 3 min; never below +29 while over the
tower ring; a charge catches a player sprinting away and ends; a missed charge gives up within 6 s; Young flies > 1.8x
Old; a body resynced at the playtest's frozen spot (-48,104,-12) is back on the orbit within 15 s; dragon yaw puts the

# farming — hand-off

Entry `com.skyclone.mod.farming.FarmingModule`. Member data key **`farming`** (`FarmData`). Data in
`plugin/src/main/resources/data/farming/` (items, recipes, mobs, spawns, shops, tables), all generated by
`python tools/gen/farming/gen_farming.py` from `research/data` (re-run it, never hand-edit the JSON). Islands:
`com.skyclone.mod.farming.gen.BarnGenerator` (`barn`) and `...DesertGenerator` (`mushroom_desert`), pure.

## What works (verified how)

Verified with `bash tools/check.sh farming` = **CHECK OK**, **9/9 JUnit tests** (`plugin/src/test/java/com/skyclone/mod/farming/FarmingPureTest.java`,
run with javac + the JUnit platform launcher; includes running BOTH generators through `Walkability` and asserting zero failed
anchors/portals, >= 10 fairy souls, no out-of-bounds writes), and `tools/preview.sh` on both islands (RESULT OK, PNGs looked at
and iterated). The server was NOT started (integrator's job) - everything Bukkit-side is compile-checked and covered by the
10 self-tests below. A review pass (2026-10-02, see "Review pass" at the end) fixed 14 bugs and added the Garden hand-shake,
Personal Bests, Turbo-crop books, the host sign-up and Digger Nim's treasure.

- **Crop breaking** (BreakHandler, priority 100) on the islands in dial `farming.islands` (hub, barn, mushroom_desert; the garden
  runs its own; on the hub only inside the zones/areas of dial `farming.hub-zones`, default the `farm` district): all 13 crops (research 08 1.2) - wheat, carrot, potato, pumpkin, melon, sugar cane, cactus, cocoa, red/brown
  mushroom, nether wart, sunflower, moonflower (blue_orchid block), wild rose (rose_bush). Only grown crops harvest (an unripe one
  flashes "still growing"); cane/cactus keep their root; tall flowers take both halves. Drops = base (research table) x
  `drops = base x (1 + floor(F/100) + chance)` (research 08 1.3), extra Seeds from wheat, Poisonous Potato chance, all given through
  `Sky.loot().give(FARMING)` in stack-sized pieces; Farming XP per BLOCK (research 02 2.4.1); `SkyEvents.Gather(FARMING, dropId, drops)`;
  objective `harvest_wheat`; break particles + sound.
- **Fortune** = player's FARMING_FORTUNE stat + crop-specific parts of the held tool: tier fortune (Mk I/II/III +10/+25/+50), tool
  level (+2/level, 50 levels, XP table from skyhanni `hoe_exp_levels`), Logarithmic Counter (+16/digit, research: 1,234,567 -> +112),
  Collection Analysis (+8/digit of the player's collection, via `Features.Collections`), Turbo-<crop> enchants and Dedication
  (enchanting module's EnchantDef values; Dedication x crop milestone from garden milestone tables), Fungi Cutter colour mode.
  `/fortune [crop]` shows the full breakdown.
- **Regrowth without random ticks**: ageable crops go to age 0 and pass through 3 visible stages back to the exact harvested block;
  block crops/plants/stacks come back after their timer. Pending regrowth is saved every 30 s and finished on shutdown; leftovers are
  restored on boot. Vanilla growth/mushroom spread is cancelled on our islands (dial).
- **Tools (48 + 3 tilling + blueprint)**: every crop tool line Mk I-III (7 hoe lines incl. Solstice Sickle for sunflower+moonflower,
  2 dicers, cactus knife, cocoa chopper, fungi cutter), rookie/promising/gardening hoes and axes, vanilla hoes (WOOD_HOE/GOLD_HOE
  registered so minion recipes resolve). Lore shows crop fortune, counter, analysis, level bar, almanacs, fungi mode. Counters/XP are
  buffered and flushed every 2 s (no lore re-render per crop). Dicer proc, Swift Slicer (+Speed while held on farming islands),
  Fungi Cutter right-click mode switch, tilling hoes (3x3, 5x5, a row - only where you may build), Farmer's Almanac (+1 FF each, max 5)
  applied from the Tool Bench. Recipes: Mk II/III copied from NEU; Mk I = Mathematical Hoe Blueprint + 8x64 of the crop (TUNE, unlock
  `collection:<crop>:5`) so Barn players can get them without the Garden.

  core calendar's realtime math; also registered as calendar event `farming_contest`, and `/sb calendar force farming_contest on`

## What is partial / not built

- **Not run on a live server.** Menus, NPC skins (mannequin + NEU texture values), regrowth timing, the contest loop and
  every wave-5 hook (accessories / pets / reforges read by id) are compile-checked, unit-tested and self-tested only.
- **The Garden gets farming's player-side crop fortune only through the held crop tool** (wave 5, dial
  `farming.garden-player-bonuses`): Personal Bests, the Pig pet's Potato Fortune and Shine Rush, and the Dark Cacao Truffle
  ride on the tool's instance `crop_fortune`, refreshed on every 2 s flush. In the Garden with a generic hoe (no crop list)
  or no farming tool they do not apply, and the first 2 s after a change use the old number. Accessories' per-crop fortune
  is NOT carried any more: the garden counts it itself since its wave 5 (`Effects.accessoryCropFortune`), so carrying it
  would pay twice (dial `farming.garden-accessory-bonuses`, default off).
- **Trapper Crest upgrade = core's recombobulator flag** (the only per-instance rarity step core has), so an upgraded
  crest cannot also be recombobulated (the reference allows both, Common -> Uncommon -> Rare). The pelt count lives in
  member data (`crestPelts`), not on the item, because a crest in the accessory bag is the accessories module's stack.
- **Overbloom -> rare crops is our formula** (+1% Harvest Fair rare-crop chance per point, dial; research names the stat,
  not the formula). Sources read: Overpriced (held), Thorny (worn armour + equipment), Freshly Baked accessories,
  Orchid Mantis pet. Crop Fever's +15 and Hedgehog's "vs pests" are enchanting's / garden's.
- Tool counter/XP is buffered for up to 2 s; it is written before the tool can leave (any inventory opening, a drop - onto
  the dropped stack - a hand swap, slot change, quit); game worlds keep inventory on death, so nothing else can take it.
- Mk I tool recipes and the Farmhand's prices are our invention (TUNE); the reference sells Mk I in the Garden's copper shop.
- Jake/Rancher axes, Windmill Operator coin shop and the Bait-maker are the shops module's NPCs (anchors provided).
- The Hall of Fame keeps real players only (top 10 per crop, one entry each) and starts empty.
- Shearing on a Sky-mob sheep relies on no other module cancelling `PlayerShearEntityEvent` for it (minions cancels
  only its own minion mobs). Not clicked in a client.
- Sunflower/moonflower/wild rose contest crops are off by default (dial `farming.contest.flowers`; research 1.16 lists
  the ten classic crops only).
- Rose Dragon's Rosy Scales (+FF per crop milestone), Elephant's Sowdust, Mosquito/Slug/Hedgehog pest perks: garden's
  systems (milestones, sowdust, pests, sprays) - not read here.

- **Harvest Fair NPC farmers farm at their own pace** (`ContestMath.GhostPace`): a short warm-up, a drifting pace
  (tiring or finishing strong) and two slow rhythms, deterministic per (fair, crop, ghost), always positive, exactly the
  final score at the end. Their scores rise smoothly second by second and the field reorders during the fair, instead of
  every ghost sitting on one even line in the same order. Live sidebar, leaderboard, early end and late ranking all read
  ghosts through `ContestMath.ghostsAt(finals, paces, t)`.
- **Harvest Fair survives restarts** (`plugins/<plugin>/farming-contest.json`, saved on start/end, every 30 s and on
  shutdown): a FORCED fair resumes if its window is still open; a fair whose window passed while the server was down is
  ranked where it stood at the last save; `finishLate` ranks a late player against the ghosts as they stood when that fair
  stopped (was: against their 20-minute finals).
- **Hall of Fame** (Harvest Fair menu, slot 38): the best real score ever in each crop, top 10, one per player, with
  bracket and date; persisted in the same file.

`Features.Objectives`, `Features.Bestiary`, `Features.Museum`**, plus Fairy Souls and Century Cakes.

`python tools/gen/progression/gen_progression.py` from `research/data` (NEU constants) — re-run it, never
hand-edit the JSON. Renames: `docs/names/progression.md` (generated by the same script).

The Leveling menu files an award under a task by the key's first `:` segment:
`skill` · `collection` · `minion` · `bank` · `fairy` · `museum` · `accessory`/`mp` · `pet` · `travel`/`warp`/`scroll` ·
`fiesta` · `fishing_festival` · `spooky` · `contest`/`jacob` · `catacombs` · `class` · `dungeon`/`floor` · `essence` ·
`slayer` · `boss_collection` · `bestiary` · `dragon` · `slayer_boss` · `kuudra` · `arachne` · `mythological` ·
`hotm`/`potm`/`powder` · `commission` · `nucleus` · `garden`/`visitor`/`crop`/`plot`/`composter`/`greenhouse` · `trophy` · `hotf` ·
`bag_upgrade` · `community` · `personal_bank` · `consumable`/`cake` · `faction`/`reputation` · `objective`/`quest` · `rift`.
Unknown prefixes land in Miscellaneous. Examples: `minion:COBBLESTONE:7`, `slayer:zombie:3`, `hotm:4`, `garden:level:5`.

**Objective ids other modules should complete** with `Sky.service(Features.Objectives.class).complete(p, id)`
(all also complete on their own; calling it just makes it instant and exact):
`talk_island_helper` (island) · `place_minion` (minions, when a minion is placed) · `deposit_coins` (bank, first deposit) ·
`talk_merchant` (shops) · `visit_bazaar` (bazaar, **only from its NPC / at the bazaar**) · `visit_auction_house` (auction, **only from its NPCs / inside the Auction House** — never on menu open, the menu opens from anywhere) ·
`harvest_wheat` (farming) · `mine_coal` (mining) · `talk_slayer_master` (slayer) · `talk_librarian` (enchanting) ·
`talk_blacksmith` (reforges) · `visit_garden` (garden). Unknown ids are recorded (so `completed()` answers) and pay nothing.
`current(p)` returns the current objective's id, or `""` when the chain is finished.

**Member data others may READ** (with their own mirror class, field names are stable):
`progression.fairy` → `{found: [ "island:anchor" ], exchanged: int}` (storage: backpack slots by souls — or simply use the
requirement `fairysouls:<n>`); `progression.level` → `{awards: {key: xp}, xp: int, emblem: String}`.

## Known risks

1. **Objective wiring depends on names other modules choose**: NPC anchors (from CONTRACT §4, safe), hub area names
   (`farm`, `graveyard`, `coal_mine`), Gather `what` strings ("COAL", "WHEAT"), the SkyBlock Menu's title containing
   "SkyBlock Menu", hub zone ids `bazaar` and `auction_house` (HubVillage `g.zone(...)`). Mismatches leave a step stuck — every such
   step is skippable, and `/sb selftest progression.objective-targets` lists unwired ones.
2. **Bestiary name-matching is heuristic**: a module that renames a reference mob AND does not set `family` gets an auto-family
   (default bracket 2 / cap 1000) instead of the reference numbers. Still counts and rewards, just not the curated numbers.
3. **Museum stores real items in profile JSON** (base64 of `serializeAsBytes`, ~1-3 KB each). A big museum is a big profile
   document. Retrieval is dupe-safe on one server thread; it has not been exercised with a real modded item.

## Round 3 fixes (playtest-endgame)

- **"OBJECTIVE COMPLETE Visit The End / The Barn / The Park..." fired while the sidebar still said "Talk to your
  Island Helper"** (a player who never talked to Gerald and warped everywhere). Root cause: every fire-and-forget
  trigger (island change, zone, NPC, command, other modules' `complete()`) credited ANY not-done step on the spot,
  box and reward included, while the sidebar shows the first un-done step - so chat and the sidebar disagreed.
  Fix: a step **ahead** of the current one is now recorded in `PData.Objectives.early` (new field, additive) with
  no box and no reward. `completed()` and the requirement `quest:<id>` still answer true at once (other modules'
  gates are unaffected). When the chain reaches it - the current step completing, a skip, an admin reset/skipto,
  a resumed skip, a login - the consecutive run of early steps is credited **in one box** ("OBJECTIVES COMPLETE",
  the step just done in white, the others "(done earlier)", summed coins and XP), then "New Objective" names the
  first step really not done. A step BEHIND the pointer (one the player skipped and then did) is still credited
  at once. Decision logic is pure in `PMath.currentObjective / objectiveReached / objectiveCatchUp`.
- Quest Log: early steps show as cyan "Done early - Rewarded when you reach it" instead of `???`.
  `/sb progression objective <p> list` marks them `early`; `complete <id>` on a step ahead says it was recorded
  early; `info` counts them; `reset` clears them; `skipto` drops the ones it marks done.
- Existing profiles: steps already credited out of order stay credited (nothing is taken back).
- Verified: `bash tools/check.sh progression` CHECK OK (183 files); unit test `outOfOrderObjectivesWaitForTheChain`
  (the playtest scenario on the real 36-step chain) + the other 5 pass (6/6). Not run on the server (forbidden).
  Integrator: fresh profile, do not talk to Gerald, warp to the Barn/Park/End - no box, sidebar stays on Gerald,
  Quest Log shows them "Done early"; talk to Gerald - one box for Gerald (+ any early run directly after it).
- Note: "Skipped Gerald" in the playtest is read as "never talked to him". If it meant the Quest Log skip, the
  sidebar should have moved on to "Break a Log"; that path was not reproduced.

# armor — hand-off

Package `com.skyclone.mod.armor` (entry `ArmorModule`). Data in `plugin/src/main/resources/data/armor/`
(`items.json` 546 pieces, `recipes.json` 321 recipes, `sets.json` 184 entries = 122 multi-piece sets + 62 lone
pieces; wave 5: `extras.json` = the Great Spook Staff, the one non-armour item a bonus needs), all generated by `python tools/gen/armor/gen_armor.py` from `research/data/items.json`, the NEU item
files and `collections.json`. Set-bonus text is hand-written in `tools/gen/armor/bonus_text.py` (our words,
research numbers). Re-run the generator after editing either file; never hand-edit the JSON.

## Round 2 fix — right-click equip (integration round 1, observed #3)
**Symptom (real client):** from the hotbar, the Superior Dragon helmet and chestplate equipped with right-click,
the leggings and boots stayed in the hotbar (combat:20 met). Not reproduced live by this fixer (no server).

**Root cause:** right-click equipping was split across two owners. Only HEAD/BLOCK helmets were swapped by armor;
every other piece was left to vanilla's item-use path (`Equippable.swapWithEquipmentSlot`), which any listener's
`setUseItemInHand(DENY)`/cancel, Paper's block-then-air interact de-dupe, or client/server prediction drift turns
into a silent no-op — and the helmet working through our path and the chestplate through vanilla's made the two
look like one mechanism. Nothing in armor guaranteed the gesture for legs/feet.

**Fix (armor files only):**
- `BonusListener.onInteract` (LOWEST) now handles **every** sky item whose category is HELMET/CHESTPLATE/LEGGINGS/
  BOOTS: denies the vanilla use (so it can never equip twice) and swaps the piece itself via `equipFromHand`
  (one item from the stack into the slot, the old worn piece back to the hand; overflow from a stacked hand goes
  to the inventory/ground). Doors/chests still open unless sneaking; pieces with a RIGHT_CLICK/SNEAK_RIGHT_CLICK
  ability keep the click for the ability; spectators ignored.
- **De-dupe** (`SetMath.sameEquipClick`): one click can arrive as a block AND an air event (same tick or straddling
  one); the second, same-slot event is swallowed so it cannot swap the piece straight back. A click on a
  DIFFERENT slot is never swallowed, so fast helmet→chest→legs→boots clicking (a bot driver) all land.
- `Worn.forget` after the swap (Worn caches per tick), then `stats().invalidate`.
- `ArmorUtil.armorIndex(category)` (pure) maps category → armour-contents index.
- Dial text of `armor.right-click-equip` updated. Two JUnit tests added: every category maps to its own slot and
  every generated piece's category is wearable; the de-dupe swallows only the same click.

**Round 2 live check:** `/sb armor give <you> superior_dragon`, put the four pieces in hotbar 1–4, press 1+RMB,
2+RMB, 3+RMB, 4+RMB (fast is fine) → all four worn, hotbar empty. Then hold a second set's leggings + RMB → they
swap with the worn leggings. Right-click a chest while holding boots → chest opens; sneak+RMB → boots equip.

## Known risks
- **(wave 5) Nether Lord edits the world**: real blocks, reverted on a timer, on chunk unload, on disable, and from the
  chunk's PDC after a crash. If a protection module ever snapshots/rolls back Crimson Isle blocks, check it ignores
  `skyclone:armor_obsidian` cells. Turn it off with `armor.ember-obsidian false`.
- **(wave 5) Spook hides the player** (`hidePlayer`) for 5 s, which also drops them from other players' tab list for
  that time. No other module hides players today; if one starts to, the end of a Spook re-shows the player.
- **Ownership overlap**: armor enables before crimson/slayer/dungeons, so any id both register keeps OUR def.
  The generator excludes SLAYER-required, KUUDRA-required, dungeon_item, STARRED_, rift and UNOBTAINABLE items
  (36 / 100 / 112 / 25 / 15); anything outside those rules that another module also claims will be ours.
- **Requirements fail closed**: a piece whose requirement prefix has no resolver (`skill`, `collection`, `hotm`,
  `garden`) cannot be worn for stats or bonuses until that module is live.
- **Stat-pass cost**: Holy Blood and Battalion scan players in the same world on every 10-tick stat pass
  (O(players²) per world); fine for tens of players, revisit for hundreds.

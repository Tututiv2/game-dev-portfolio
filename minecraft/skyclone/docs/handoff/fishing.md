## Known risks
1. **Bobber in lava** may be removed by vanilla before we freeze it (see partial). Water fishing does not depend on it.
2. **Vanilla bite suppression** relies on `FishHook.setWaitTime(72000,72000)` + cancelling `BITE`/`LURED`; if Paper ignores it on a hook already counting down, a vanilla bite would just be cancelled (harmless) but double "!!!" sounds could appear.
3. **Data volume**: 223 items / 84 mobs registered in enable(); items copied from materials' fishing family keep materials' names. Pets/enchanting must register the pet/book ids we drop or those drops are pruned (logged at boot).
4. NPC skins reuse community-repo NPC textures (same practice as the shops module) — swap if the owner wants original art.

## Review pass (2026-10-02)
A second agent reviewed the module end to end and changed:
- **Vanilla loot on game worlds is impossible now.** A `CAUGHT_FISH` (a vanilla bite that slipped through, or a hook that is not ours) is cancelled and the hook removed, so no vanilla books/saddles/name tags. A FISHING_ROD-category cast while the profile is not ready is cancelled. Non-rod items with a rod material (Grappling Hook, whips) are left to their own module, as before.
- **Bait is consumed on landing**, not on cast (a cast onto land or the wrong liquid no longer eats one). Sacks are a last fallback source (`Features.Sacks`), and owned-bait counts in menus include them.
- **Fishing Bag hardening:** a stored id that is no longer a registered bait is shown as a locked "Unknown Bait" slot instead of being silently erased on the next save; a stored count above one stack (old data) is clamped and the rest handed back in the same tick; a bag closed without a profile discards its shown copies (handing them back would duplicate the stored bag) and logs it.
- **Rod parts:** the old part is only returned once the new one is verified on the rod, and a removed part only once it is verified gone (no part printing if an update does not take, e.g. on a stackable rod). The Tackle-smith shows a uuid-free display copy of the rod instead of a clone of the unique item.
- **Starter rod:** the "already given" flag is set when the rod is handed over, not when the dialogue starts (leaving mid-talk no longer loses it; a re-click cannot get two).
- **Winter gifts were farmable** (10% per hit, unlimited, so a weak weapon printed gifts): now capped per creature (`gifts.max-per-creature` 5) and the chance is a dial.
- **Hotspots never load chunks** to look for water (the 40-attempt search ran every 5 s per island on the main thread and could load chunks); unloaded columns are skipped.
- **Gusher window** now matches research 07 3.7 (10-15 min after an eruption; the dials claimed 7/16 were research).
- **Sulphur Skitter:** the angler OR the bobber within 4 blocks of sulphur (sponge) counts.
- Sidebar: the festival/hotspot lines only show while a rod is in hand (five shared lines); hotspot line uses a short label instead of cutting a colour code in half.
- Rare catches also show a title (respecting the `fishing.sea_creature_alerts` setting).

## Playtest-loops fix pass (2026-10-03): Sea Witch deaths
The playtest left "kept fishing with a Sea Witch alive: died once, ended at 2 HP once" as unverified after core's fix #5
(`MobListener.onMobPotion`: a live sky mob's harmful splash hits once through the pipeline, no vanilla effect). That fix
holds only while the thrower is alive, and the same bug class (vanilla side-effects that tick as Defense-ignoring vanilla
damage) had other doors on sea creatures. New `SeaCreatureGuard` (listener, registered in `FishingModule`) closes them:
- **Orphaned shots.** Every projectile a sea creature launches is tagged (`skyclone:fishing_shot`, set on
  `ProjectileLaunchEvent`). A tagged splash never applies a vanilla effect (HIGHEST, after core has charged a live
  thrower) - before this, killing the witch while her bottle was in the air (the normal case) let vanilla Instant Damage /
  Poison land, because core no longer sees a live shooter. A tagged arrow/shot whose creature is gone fizzles (LOW, before
  core's environment fallback charged it as PROJECTILE damage ignoring Defense).
- **On-hit vanilla effects:** Wither from Wither-Skeleton types (Fire Eel, Thunder...), Poison arrows (Bogged), Slowness
  arrows (Stray types). An `ATTACK`/`ARROW` effect on a player in the same tick a sea creature (or its shot) hit them is cancelled.
- **Elder Guardian curse** (Mining Fatigue to players within 50 blocks, slows the vanilla swing): cancelled near a live
  elder-guardian sea creature (PLUGIN/COMMAND causes untouched).
- **Guardian thorns:** hitting a Guardian with its spikes out sent vanilla THORNS damage from it, which core routes as a
  full mob hit (def.damage per swing). Cancelled before core.
- Self-test **`fishing.witch-potions-neutralised`** (13th): spawns each WITCH-type sea creature (Sea Witch, Fireproof Witch)
  for one tick above the main world's spawn and fires a harmful splash from it, live thrower and dead thrower; fails if any
  intensity survives. Verified: `check.sh fishing` OK, unit tests 11/11. **Not run live** (no server/client this pass) -
  the next playtest should re-run "keep fishing with a Sea Witch alive" and kill her mid-throw.
- Not changed (core's lane, noted for the integrator): core's own `onMobPotion` and the CombatListener thorns/arrow
  routing would cover every sky mob, not just sea creatures, if moved there.

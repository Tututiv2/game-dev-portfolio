# mines — hand-off

Three islands (Gold Mine, Deep Caverns, Dwarven Mines), their mobs, spawns, travel NPCs and the
Dwarven Mines pass. Mining SYSTEMS (breaking, regen, powder, HOTM, commissions, the Forge) belong to
the mining module; it finds everything through the anchors and `ores.*` areas listed below.

## What works (verified how)
- **Three pure generators** in `com.skyclone.mod.mines.gen` (no Bukkit): `GoldMineGenerator`,
  `DeepCavernsGenerator`, `DwarvenMinesGenerator`, plus the shared `CaveGrid` (column-based caves that
  only write a rock shell, so the 440-block Dwarven Mines is 2.7M blocks, not 20M) and `MineKit`
  (supports, lanterns on chains, rails, platforms, framed portals, fairy-soul bookkeeping).
  `bash tools/preview.sh com.skyclone.mod.mines.gen.<Gen>` → **RESULT: OK, zero unreachable anchors and
  portals, zero floating blocks** for all three (gen 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 s; 0.89M / 0.81M / 2.72M blocks).
  Underground islands render as their roof in preview.sh, so `bash tools/gen/mines/cutaway.sh <Gen>
  lo:hi:name ... [crop=x1,z1,x2,z2]` renders horizontal slices (previews/<island>/cut_*.png). I looked at
  every level.
  - **Gold Mine** (−100..100): floating island with a sunlit open pit — five terraced benches and a
    spiral haul road (stair-cut), ore-streaked walls (gold/iron/coal), timber scaffolds with ladders
    between benches, a crane over the rim, a headframe at the pit floor, a lapis tunnel + grotto (west)
    and a gold gallery (north) with mine supports and rails, and a miners' camp on the south rim
    (gold forge, iron smithy, cottages, store, gold obelisk at spawn). 13 fairy souls.
  - **Deep Caverns** (−74..74, y 12..170): six stacked cave levels (Gunpowder Mines 150, Lapis Quarry 124,
    Pigmen's Den 98, Slime Hollow 72, Diamond Reserve 46, Obsidian Sanctuary 18), each its own palette,
    ores, lights and props; a spiral haul road walks from every level to the next; a caged lift shaft runs
    through the middle with a Lift Operator and a SAFE station on every floor; arrival hall with portals
    to the Gold Mine and the Hub; the Sanctuary has a pillared hall and the obsidian gate to the Dwarven
    Mines. 24 fairy souls.
  - **Dwarven Mines** (−224..224, y 74..210): 15 districts joined by graded passages — Dwarven Village
    (plaza, Heart of the Mountain crystal monument, commission board, 12 houses incl. Tavern and Grand
    Library, market stalls, gate with portals to the Hub and Deep Caverns), Royal Palace (gold-banded keep
    with towers, throne room, barracks) across a lava chasm on the diagonal **Palace Bridge**, The Forge
    (open hall, lava chimney), Ramparts Quarry, Goblin Burrows (tents, campfires), Lava Springs (lava pools,
    basalt), Upper Mines, Royal Mines, Cliffside Veins, Far Reserve, Great Ice Wall (an actual ice ridge with
    two gaps), The Mist (sunken bowl), Deepking's Gateway (amethyst portal to the Crystal Hollows), Dwarven
    Base Camp (portal to the Glacite Tunnels). Mining districts are coated in mithril by 3D noise clusters
    (grade 1 gray wool/cyan terracotta, grade 2 prismarine family, grade 3 light blue wool) with 1.2%
    titanium (polished diorite). 13 minecart stations. 20 fairy souls.
- **Mobs** (`data/mines/mobs.json`, 19 defs since wave 3) and **spawns** (`spawns.json`, 22 rules), generated by
  `tools/gen/mines/gen_mines_data.py`, which parses the stat tables in research 03 §9.2/§10.2 (HP, damage,
  XP, coins) — not hand-typed. `family` = the bestiary family ids from neu-repo so progression's bestiary
  matches them. TUNE values are flagged in each entry's `_tune` field.
- **Behaviours** (`MineMobs`): Sneaky Creeper is invisible and its fuse is replaced by our own blast
  (def damage in a dial radius, no block damage, dies without loot); Ghost = invisible charged creeper,
  takes only melee/ferocity/true damage, adds 100 true damage per hit, never explodes, 0.01% 1M-coin burst;
  Knifethrower / Fireslinger throw dodgeable knives / fireballs on a cooldown (wave 3) (line of sight, never into SAFE zones; fire sets
  you alight); Ice Walker slows; Emerald Slimes have sizes 2/3/4 and never split. Explosions on the three
  islands never break blocks.
- **Travel**: `/mines` overview (three islands with live requirement state, lift, minecarts, pass),
  `/lift [layer]` + Lift Operators (only to layers you walked into — dial), `/minecart [station]` + Ticket
  Masters (fare dials, free by default; cooldown dial). Layers are marked visited on zone entry.
- **Dwarven Mines pass**: Delver Thornley at `npc.delver` (Obsidian Sanctuary) gives it at Mining XII
  (dial). The warp requirement is `skill:mining:12 & mines:pass` (dial). Requirement prefix `mines`
  (`mines:pass`, `mines:layer:<n>`) is registered here.
- **The Mist** draws fog particles round players in it (wave 3; blindness is an off-by-default dial), cleared on leaving / island change / login.
- **Unit tests** `plugin/src/test/java/com/skyclone/mod/mines/MinesIslandsTest.java`: 11 tests, all pass (wave 3)
  (run outside gradle with the JUnit launcher): walkability OK for all three real generators, no writes
  outside bounds, every required anchor/portal/ore area present, 10–40 souls each, every spawn rule
  points at a real area and mob, mob numbers equal the research table, zone→layer mapping, fares.
- `bash tools/check.sh mines` → **CHECK OK**.

## Fix 2026-10-03 (playtest-loops: "Minecart to Goblin Burrows drops you among ~30 goblins and a Lv200 Cutthroat")
- **Root cause:** the eight minecart stations out in mining districts (Ramparts Quarry, Goblin Burrows, Lava
  Springs, Upper Mines, Royal Mines, Cliffside Veins, Far Reserve, Great Ice Wall) sat inside their district's
  plain (non-SAFE) zone, and the station search places them within 10-60% of the district radius, i.e. inside the
  mob area. Goblin Burrows' station is 4 blocks from the district centre. Only the five town stations were safe.
- **Fix:** `DwarvenMinesGenerator.stationZones()` gives every station whose Ticket Master is not already in a SAFE
  zone its own SAFE zone `station_<id>` ("Goblin Burrows Station", the district's colour), 11x8x11 round the
  Ticket Master (`STATION_SAFE_RADIUS` 5; the platform reaches 3 out). Core's rules then do the rest: no spawns
  inside, mobs never target or hurt you there, and any that walk in are leashed back out. The zones are added
  AFTER the district zones so mining's commission naming (first zone containing an ore area's centre) still
  resolves to the district — checked: all nine `ores.*` areas still name their district. The station boxes hold a
  few ore faces at most; those are unmineable unless mining's `allowSafeZones` dial is on.
- **Generator `version()` 1 → 2**, so the Dwarven Mines rebuilds on the next boot (~2.7M blocks, ~90 s) and the
  persisted layout picks up the new zones. Blocks are unchanged; only the layout grew.
- **Tests:** new unit test `everyStationAndLiftStopIsSafe` (every `station.*` / `npc.ticket.*` anchor in the
  Dwarven Mines and every `lift.n` in the Deep Caverns is in a SAFE zone by the smallest-box rule) — 9/9 pass.
  New live self-test `mines.stations-safe` (same check through `Sky.worlds().zoneAt`; fails with "rebuild the
  island?" if a stale layout is loaded). `bash tools/check.sh mines` → CHECK OK.
- **Not changed (worth a look; DONE in wave 3, see below):** the Goblin Burrows still has ~31 goblins including the Lv200 Cutthroat
  (60k HP / 2,500 dmg, data marked TUNE) and the station still sits near the district's centre — stepping off the
  platform is a real fight by design. Not re-verified in a live client.

## Known risks
- **Build size/time**: the Dwarven Mines is ~2.7M blocks (≈90 s at core's 32k blocks/s); Gold Mine and
  Deep Caverns ~0.9M each. First boot builds all three.
- **Vanilla AI vs custom rules**: creepers (Sneaky, Ghost) rely on ExplosionPrimeEvent being fired before
  core removes them; ghosts are creepers so if some other code lets them prime they will still not break
  blocks (blockList cleared on my islands) but could damage via vanilla. Baby-zombie goblins may be fast.
- **Warp requirements are read once at boot** (dials changed live take effect after a restart); the
  `skill:` prefix must be resolved by the skills module or the Deep Caverns/Dwarven warps fail closed.

### Done
- **Real minecart rides** (`Rides.java`, `gen/RailPlanner.java`, `gen/RailRoute.java`). The Dwarven Mines now has
  **laid vanilla track** from the depot (village station) to all 13 stations: a Dijkstra over the finished cavern
  that obeys what rails can do (rise/fall one block, never turn on a slope, never rail a one-block dip; turns cost
  extra so lines run in long straights), rolls only over solid floor with 4 blocks of headroom, and keeps off
  portals, buildings, the plaza and NPCs. The union of lines is a tree (junctions, no parallel spaghetti). Rails sit
  on a stone-brick bed, which no mining block list contains, so nobody can dig a rail loose. Each line is stored as
  layout data `rail.<station>`; a ride A->B is A's line back to the fork then B's line out
  (`RailRoute.join`). The cart is a real Minecart riding an invisible block display that is teleported along the
  track every tick with client interpolation (smooth, and no vanilla rail/gravity/WASD physics can fight it); it
  follows the vanilla wheel height (ascending rails, corner-cut curves), accelerates and brakes into the buffer.
- **Real lift rides**: the cage (a 5x5 plank-floor display with two chains) moves up and down the Deep Caverns shaft.
  The shaft now runs on below the Obsidian Sanctuary (`SHAFT_BOTTOM` 4), and the lift's **7th stop is the Dwarven
  Mines** (reference: Lift Operator -> The Lift): down the Deep Caverns shaft into the dark, the island change, then
  down **The Lift**'s own 54-block shaft into the Dwarven village. The Dwarven Lift Operator takes you back up to any
  visited layer. Riding PAST a layer does not mark it visited.
- Rides: **sneak = hop out at the destination** (the reference is instant, so waiting is always optional); riders
  take no damage of any kind and are never targeted (core damage events, vanilla damage, knives/fireballs/blasts);
  a warp, death or logout ends the ride where it is; disable puts riders at their destination. Missing layout data

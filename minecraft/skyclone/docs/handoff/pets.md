# pets — hand-off

Module `com.skyclone.mod.pets.PetsModule` (id `pets`). Provides `Features.Pets`.
Data: `plugin/src/main/resources/data/pets/*.json`, generated by `py -3 tools/gen/pets/gen_pets.py` from
`research/data/neu-repo` (petnums/pets constants, per-rarity pet files, katgrade recipes, Zog's shop) and
`research/data/items.json`. Our wording/effects live in `tools/gen/pets/abilities.py` and `pet_items.py`.

## What works (verified how)

Verified by `bash tools/check.sh pets` (CHECK OK) and 14 unit tests in
`plugin/src/test/java/com/skyclone/mod/pets/PetMathTest.java` (all pass, run against the SHIPPED json files with the
JUnit platform launcher - see Wave 3 for the command), plus 9 live self-tests for the integrator. Learn / summon /
Combat-XP levelling / Taming were seen live in the 2026-10-03 playtest (docs/PLAYTEST.md); wave 3 is compile + unit only.

- **All 88 pet types, every rarity they exist in** (`TYPE;n` item ids exactly as neu-repo: `BEE;4` = Legendary Bee,
  `ENDER_DRAGON;3` = Epic Ender Dragon), head textures, our names for the proper-noun ones.
- **Exact XP curve**: shared PET_LEVELS table at the rarity offset (C0 U6 R11 E16 L20 M20), Bingo offset 0,
  Reindeer x2 XP, Golden/Jade/Rose Dragon to level 200 (+100 x 1,886,700). Unit-tested against every row of the
  research §6.2 table (Common 100 = 5,624,785 ... Legendary 100 = 25,353,230; GD 200 = 214,023,230).
- **Stats and ability numbers interpolate by level** (`lvl1 -> lvl100`, linear, per rarity, straight from petnums);
  dragons use the 101:200 stat curve. Tier Boost raises the effective rarity and re-computes the level from the same XP.
- **Pet XP from skill XP** (`SkyEvents.SkillXpGain`, MONITOR): own-type x1, other x1/3, Fishing x1.5,
  Alchemy/Enchanting /4 for non-matching, Taming x1.01/level, held Exp Boost items, mayor perk bonus; Carpentry,
  Taming, Runecrafting, Social feed nothing. Kuudra-type pet only gains on the Crimson Isle.
- **Taming XP**: the summoned pet's XP x `pets.taming-xp-ratio` (TUNE 0.25) via `SkillService.addXp(TAMING)`.
- **Exp Share**: 1 slot (+2 with the sharing perk), rate = Taming 0.2%/lvl (max 12) + Exp Share item 15 + perk 10, cap 47.
- **Level-up chat message**, live pet lore on items and menu icons (stat lines, abilities with numbers at the pet's
  level, held item, progress bar, total XP, candy used).
- **Floating pet**: ItemDisplay of the pet head bobbing at the left shoulder + TextDisplay `&8[Lvl 23] &6Bee`,
  follows across worlds, hidden for viewers who toggle "Hide Pets".
- **Pets menu** (`/pets`, SkyBlock Menu -> Pets): paged, sort (rarity/level/skill/name), left-click summon/despawn,
  right-click details, Convert-to-item toggle, Hide Pets, Exp Sharing, Autopet Rules, Pet Score -> Magic Find.
  **Pet details**: summon, held item (give from inventory / take back), feed candy, Exp Share toggle, convert.
- **Right-click a pet item** = learn it into the menu (dupe guard: the item's uuid becomes the pet's id; the same
  uuid twice is refused). **Right-click a pet item (held item)** with a pet summoned = give it (old one returned).
  **Right-click candy/Pet Cake** = feed (Simple 20k, Great 200k, Superb 750k, Ultimate 1.5M, Cake 10k; 10-candy cap).
- **Held items**: all 95 reference pet items (+ Super-Boost, Bigger Teeth, Serrated Claws, Gold Claws...) with
  effects: flat stats, pet-stat multipliers (Minos/"Labyrinth" 33.3%, Forgemaster 50%, Textbook, Remedies, Claws),
  XP boosts, Tier Boost, Exp Share, Quick Claw, knockback immunity, Reaper Gem, pet-only restrictions.
- **Pet Score**: sum of best rarity per unique type (C1..M6) -> Magic Find table, +3 SkyBlock XP per new point
  (`Features.SkyLevel.award`, keyed `pets.score.<n>`, never pays twice).
- **Pet Sitter** (NPC `pets_sitter` at hub `npc.pet_sitter`, `/petsitter`): all 217 neu-repo upgrade steps
  (coins + items + real time, Taming 10/20/25 gates for Epic/Legendary/Mythic), pet leaves the menu while upgrading,
  claim / cancel (no refund) / skip time with Sitter's Daisy (1 day) and Sitter's Bouquet (5 days).
  **Pet Supplies** shop inside it: Zog's real list (coins from neu-repo) + the Sitter-only upgrade materials
  (dippers, pig ears, frog treats, plushies, cauldrons, sloth claws, griffin stones, mythic upgrade items) at TUNE prices.
  **Pet Collector** inside it: give one of the 10 research pets each for +1 Taming cap (50 -> 60, `SkillService.setCap`).
- **Autopet** (`/autopet`): up to 10 "arrive on island X -> summon pet Y" rules (`SkyEvents.IslandChange`).
- **Abilities that DO something** (interpreter in `PetEffects.java`, effects are data): flat/percent/conditional stats
  (island, mining islands, night, festival events, HP thresholds, near water, standing still, sneaking, rain, dungeon,
  zone keyword), stat-from-stat conversions (Elephant, Blue Whale, Ankylosaurus, Giraffe, Golden Dragon, Mooshroom,
  Orchid Mantis, Snail, Mammoth), per skill level / HOTM level / collection digit, nearby players / same pet / mobs,
  kill stacks (Horse, Skeleton Horse, Spider, Tarantula), bow stacks, cast buffs, jump buff, auras (Parrot, Turtle),
  festival/rain/year base-stat boosts, Bingo Chimera, Tyrant; damage vs mob families (ender, undead, spider, wolf,
  wither, slime, spooky, pest, Kuudra...), damage taken reductions, missing-HP, far/near, isolated, retaliation,
  monster level, bank-scaled (Golden Dragon), arrows, Last Stand, Rebirth (cheat death), Fourth Ember DoT, Withering
  Touch, Snow Cannon (+50% double at Mythic), Ricochet, Hurl, slow on hit, stun, Whiteout aura, Prism Beam laser
  (Guardian), Bone Sentry (Skeleton), Tail Club, knockback immunity, fall damage reduction, Trample, Overheal shield,
  Hard Shell vitality; Combat XP multipliers, coins per kill / per level, skill-XP bonuses, drop-on-gather (Honey Jar,
  mushrooms, cheese/dung, jelly/plant matter, end stone, enchanted mining mats), drop-on-kill, double drops, extra
  collection items (Black Cat, Bingo), no coin loss on death (Phoenix), mana cost cuts, free casts, weapon cooldown cuts,
  teleport-ability weapon damage, item buffs for the reference weapons (Yeti Sword, AOTD, Pigman Sword, Ink Wand,
  Aspect of the Jerry by id), axe/drill/melee-category buffs, armour-set percentage buffs by id keyword, double potato

## Wave 4 fixes (2026-10-04, from the wave4-loops playtest)

Verified: `bash tools/check.sh pets` CHECK OK; 16/16 unit tests (2 new) via the JUnit platform launcher; data regenerated
(`gen_pets.py`; the only change in `pets.json` is the Armadillo ride lore). Not run on the server (this phase forbids it).

- **Pet Score SkyBlock XP is one line per rise** (PLAYTEST open issue 6). `updateScore` paid one keyed award per score
  point, so learning two pets printed `+3 SkyBlock XP (Pet Score)` four times in normal play, not only on admin grants.
  Now ONE award per rise: key `pets.score.<new score>`, XP = 3 x points gained (`PetMath.scoreAwardXp`), reason
  `Pet Score <n>`. Same total XP. Keys never overlap because `bestScore` only rises; players holding the old per-point
  keys `pets.score.1..n` are unaffected (the next rise starts above them). Residual edge: if `bestScore` were ever lost
  while the progression awards survived, the next rise could overpay the gap once (the old per-point keys could not).
- **Armadillo mount speed** (PLAYTEST open issue 11: Lv1 with ~350 Speed ran 21.7 b/s). Root cause: the full 1.6x
  multiplier applied at level 1, Speed was not clamped to the stat cap, and the hard cap was attribute 0.6 (~26 b/s).
  Now `PetMath.mountAttr`: your own walking pace at Lv1 (Speed/100 x 0.1, Speed clamped to [100, `stats.speed-cap`]),
  rising linearly to the data's 1.6x at Lv100 (levels past 100 count as 100), never past dial `pets.ride-max-speed`
  (15 b/s ~ the fastest vanilla horse). Lv1 at 350 Speed = 15 b/s (was 21.7); Lv100 at 100 Speed = 6.9 b/s (unchanged
  from the wave-3 live reading). Lore now says "faster the more Speed you have and the higher its level"
  (`tools/gen/pets/abilities.py`). Self-test `pets.ride-data` also asserts the Lv1/350-Speed case under the live dials.
  Not seen in a client yet: ride a Lv1 Armadillo with high Speed and time it (expect <= 15 b/s).

## Known risks

1. **Partly live-tested** (2026-10-03 playtest: learn, menu, summon, levelling). Wave 3 (riding, the wisp pool,
   settings polling, Mite Lure spawns) and wave 5 (morph, cheese, parked rock, burrowing, skins, name prefix) have not
   been seen in a client. Burrowing fires synthetic `BlockDamageEvent`s into mining's dig engine (it needs the rider's
   crosshair on the block, within reach); if mining ever changes how a dig starts, the Armadillo simply digs nothing
   (`/sb pets tunnel <p>` prints the energy; it only drops when a block really broke). Start with `/sb pets give <p> BEE LEGENDARY 100`, `/pets`, summon,
   `/sb pets stats <p>`, `/sb selftest pets`.
2. **Mob matching for "vs Ender/undead/spider..." is by keyword** on mob id/family/slayer/name/entity type
   (`PetEffects.matchesKey`, unit-tested against real ids). A mob module that names things unusually will not get the
   bonus. Wave 3 TIGHTENED `pest` (only `pest` now - `mite`/`fly` hit endermites and dragonflies) and `spooky` (no
   `witch`/`bat` - they hit sea witches, cinderbats and every BAT entity), and taught `bal` our name `magmaw`.
3. **Stat feedback**: stat-from-stat effects read the PREVIOUS stat snapshot (`Sky.stats().get`), so they lag one
   recompute (0.5 s) and two pets' worth of conversion can never loop - but a percent bonus on top of a conversion
   could be off by one cycle right after a gear swap.
4. Pet item ids contain `;` — if another module validates ids with `[A-Z0-9_:]` only, pets will be rejected there.
5. The Sitter-only material prices (10k / 50k / 250k / 1M / 5M by rarity) are TUNE: the reference sources them from
   events this server may not have.

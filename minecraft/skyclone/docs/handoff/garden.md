# garden — hand-off

Package `com.skyclone.mod.garden` (entry `GardenModule`). Provides **`Features.Garden`** and the requirement prefix
**`garden`** (`garden:<level>`, `garden:unique:<n>`, `garden:plots:<n>`). Data in `plugin/src/main/resources/data/garden/*.json`,
all generated by `python tools/gen/garden/gen_garden.py` from `research/data` (NEU `constants/garden.json` + `skymart.json`,
SkyHanni `constants/Garden.json`, `items.json`) — re-run it, never hand-edit the JSON. Renames: `docs/names/garden.md`
(generated by the same script, 200 rows).

## Review pass (second agent, 2026-10-02) - what changed

Re-verified: `bash tools/check.sh garden` = **CHECK OK (189 files)**; `GardenPureTest` **6/6 pass** (one test added), compiled
and run with JUnit 5.11.4 against api+core+gen+garden in a private temp folder (no gradle). Every id the garden's data
references was cross-checked against every module's `data/*/items.json`: all exist except `GREEN_BANDANA`, `LUCKY_CLOVER`,
`RECORD_13` (visitor wants / a bonus, skipped at runtime while missing). Nothing has run live.

Bugs fixed:
1. **Item multiplication by place-break (a dupe).** Melon, pumpkin, mushrooms, sunflower, moonflower and wild rose are
   ripe the moment they are placed, so place-then-break paid Farming Fortune drops instantly and forever (x4 per item
   at 300 FF), and a sugar cane / cactus stacked on another and broken returned 2 x fortune. Now a placed (or
   right-click-planted) ripe crop must grow for one regrow period before it can be harvested (`CropListener.fresh`,
   "Ready in Ns"), and stacking cane/cactus on cane/cactus is refused (plant on the ground; it grows on the timer).
2. **Floating regrowth.** The regrow queue placed cane/flowers/melons back even when the soil under them had been dug out,
   and they popped into free items. `Growth.supported()` now requires a cane/cactus segment on the same plant, a flower top
   on its bottom, anything else on a solid block.
3. **Harvested melons/mushrooms/flowers were lost for good** when everyone left the Garden (the chunk unloaded and the queue
   gave up after an hour) and at every restart (the queue is in memory). Due entries in unloaded chunks now load that chunk
   off-thread (`getChunkAtAsync`) and grow; `GardenModule.disable()` grows everything still pending (`Growth.finishAll`).
4. **/plottp skipped the Garden warp requirement** (SkyBlock Level 5): `Desk.teleport` now checks `garden.requirement` when
   the player is not already in their Garden (admin `/sb garden tp` bypasses it on purpose).
5. **Offline visitors filled every slot at once.** After the offline catch-up, `nextVisitorAt` was hours in the past, so the
   first online tick added visitors until the Garden was full, ignoring the slower offline rate. The online clock now
   restarts from what is left of the current offline interval.
6. **Pest kinds were not saved**, so a Fly that spawned came back as a random pest after a relog. `PlotState.pestTypes`
   (new field, additive) records the kind with the count; bodies are re-created as the same kinds; kills remove that kind.
7. **Plot price could change between the confirm and the purchase** (a co-op member buying a plot of the same group): the
   confirm now re-prices and refuses if it moved.
8. **Presets**: a preset on an instance with no world yet kept the coins and left the plot "busy" for ever - now refunded;
   the crop-level gate is re-checked when the preset is laid (it was only checked when the menu was drawn).
9. **Plot Icon menu** put 21 icons in a 4-row menu, overwriting Close and losing one icon behind Back - now 5 rows.
   The visitor offer's "Request Details" covered Close (slot 22) - moved to slot 4.
10. **Overgrowth that is a locked crop** (wild moonflowers / blue orchids, wetland sugar cane) could not be broken or
    scythed below its Garden level, so a plot could not be fully cleared - it now clears with no drops. The scythe also
    left the top half of tall grass / ferns floating - both halves go now.
11. A trap picked up with bait in it lost the bait - it is returned. The Pest Radio kept boosting a vinyl you had sold - it
    stops when nobody holding the radio owns it. The Pest Radio menu had no way back - Go Back to Pests.
12. `garden.requirement` was read once at boot - it now re-registers the warp when the dial changes.

Breadth added:
- **Visitors can ask for two crops** (research 08 1.10 "1-2 item types"): rare+ visitors, more often at higher levels
  (`VisitorState.item2/amount2`, additive fields; TUNE). Accept takes both; the offer, list and admin output show both.
- **Copper Market bulk buy**: right-click a stackable item with no trade-in (Compost, Biofuel, sprays, repellents...) to buy
  1-640 at once with one confirmation.
- `/plottp barn` (or `0`) and the alias **`/tptoplot`** (research 08 1.5).
- Self-test **`garden.block-names`**: every plot preset and every barn skin is built into a canvas and each block string is
  checked with `Bukkit.createBlockData` (the Placer silently turns a rejected string into stone).

3. **Greenhouse skins** (cosmetic). The greenhouse moved out of `GardenGenerator` into `GreenhouseBuilder` (pure, same pattern as
   `BarnBuilder`): 9 skins (Greenhouse, Conservatory, Bamboo Pavilion, Tropical Glasshouse, Copper Atrium, Blossom House, Moonlit

## Known risks

   expect a multi-second hitch while it runs (admin-only, but do not run it on a busy server).

1. **First visit builds ~1.14M blocks per profile** (480x480 garden, 5 layers deep). At core's 12 ms/tick that is roughly 30-40 s of
   "Warping..." the first time, and world storage per profile. If it is too slow, lower the plot size in `PlotGrid.SIZE` (everything
   derives from it) and bump `GardenGenerator.version()`.
2. **Never run live**: per-instance NPC copies (`spawnAt`), pest bodies hovering by teleport with gravity off, the hold-right-click vacuum
   cadence, the block-budget `Placer` (presets, barn skins), and menus driven by a real client are verified by compile + unit tests +
   preview only.

   deterministic) and chip effects are `TUNE` dials/data; Garden XV needs many hundreds of visitor offers plus milestones.

## Market gate fix (2026-10-04, wave-4 note #14)
The Copper Market's "Requires Garden N" was a hand-typed `LEVEL_GATE` table in `tools/gen/garden/gen_garden.py` that nothing checked
against the items' own `Requires Garden Level N` footers: Blossom Bracelet/Belt/Necklace/Cloak were gated VIII-XI in the shop but 6-9 on
the item (the reference's tags, research 06); Lotus and the Garden Vacuum/Sprayonator went the other way (buyable at I, usable only at

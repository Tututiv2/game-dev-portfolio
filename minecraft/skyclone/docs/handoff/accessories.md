# accessories — hand-off

Package `com.skyclone.mod.accessories` (entry `AccessoriesModule`). Data in `resources/data/accessories/`
(generated by `py -3 tools/gen/accessories/gen_accessories.py` from research/data — never hand-edit the JSON).
Provides `Features.Accessories` and `Features.Equipment`; owns the requirement prefix `mp:<n>`.

## What works (verified how)

Verified by `bash tools/check.sh accessories` -> **CHECK OK**, and by **20 JUnit tests** in
`plugin/src/test/java/com/skyclone/mod/accessories/` (`AccMathTest`, `AccDataFilesTest`, `SellCapTest`), all green when run with
plain javac and the JUnit launcher. **None of it has run on a live server yet.** Run the 8 self-tests below first.

- **Items (586 registered here).** 423 accessories, 147 equipment pieces (necklace / cloak / belt / gloves;
  bracelets go in the gloves slot), 22 power stones, 11 enrichments plus the enrichment swapper. Ids are the
  reference ids. Names follow `docs/names/accessories.md`. Every description is written by our generator, so no
  reference lore text ships. Stats come from the NEU lore stat lines, which are more complete than the API.
  Requirements are converted to keys (`slayer:zombie:7`, `hotm:5`, `skill:combat:22`, `garden:N`).
- **267 recipes** (crafting and forge) for our results, with collection, slayer or HOTM unlocks taken from the
  NEU crafttext.
- **Families.** These come from NEU `talisman_upgrades` (Talisman → Ring → Artifact → Relic, the 29-step campfire
  badges, wedding rings, piggy banks and so on). Only the best of each family counts: highest rank first, then
  higher effective rarity, so a recombobulated copy beats a plain one. Duplicates and lower tiers are listed as
  "not counted" in the bag's info icon. The 28 Rift-only or retired curios (NEU `ignored_talisman`) give no
  Accessory Power.
- **Accessory Power (MP).** Per rarity: 3/5/8/12/16/22/28, Special 3, Very Special 5, all of them dials.
  Recombobulation raises it through `Sky.items().rarity()`. The Hegemony Artifact counts double. A consumed
  Rift Prism adds +11. Dungeon accessories count double while `Dungeons.inDungeon`. You earn 1 SkyBlock XP per
  point of MP the first time you reach it (keys `accessories:mp:N`).
- **Powers.** There are 32: 10 starter powers (5 free, 5 at Combat 15, from research/01 §20.3) and 22 stone
  powers whose numbers are parsed from each stone's "at 250 power" lore and divided by M(250). Each one gives
  `base × M(MP) × accessories.power-scale` plus its flat unique bonus. You learn a stone power by consuming 9
  stones (a dial), either by right-clicking or from the Power Stones menu. Learning pays 15 SkyBlock XP once.
  The power's Combat requirement is checked when you learn it, select it, and while it applies.
- **The formula.** `M = 29.97·ln(0.0019·MP+1)^1.2`, pinned by tests: M(250) = 9.642 and M(1000) = 32.312.
  Displaced Leech gives Strength 138.85 at 250 MP, and Fortuitous gives 32 Health at 250 MP.
- **Tuning.** You get 1 point per 10 MP across 8 stats; the value of a point is a dial for each stat. Left-click
  adds 1, right-click removes 1, and shift changes 10 at a time. If your MP drops, only the first N points count,
  in a fixed stat order. There are 5 templates: the first is free and the others cost 10,000 Bits each.
- **Accessory Bag** (`/accessories`). Pages of 45 slots, stored as base64 ItemStacks in member data, so reforges,
  recombs and enrichments survive. Slot sources:
  - base 9
  - Redstone collection II–IX: +6 each
  - six SkyBlock-level milestones: +2 each (these replace the reference's paid/timed account upgrades)
  - a one-time redstone exchange: +4
  - up to 99 bought pairs from "Bagwright Jory", costing 5,360·n² coins (≈1.76B for all 99)

  That gives 271 slots maximum. Anything that is not an accessory bounces back to the player.
  Right-clicking an accessory in your hand drops it into the first free slot.
- **Equipment** (`/equipment`). Four storage slots next to a read-only view of your armour, your stats and your
  pet, and `Features.Equipment.worn` feeds core's equipment stat provider. You can also right-click a piece in
  your hand to equip it, swapping it with the piece you had on.
- **Live effects (227 items; 21 of them moved from lore-only to live in wave 3, see below).**
  - **Conditional stats:** day/night crystals, campfire badges while burning, the village / wood / mine / farming
    talismans by zone or island, glacial, junk, the atmospheric filter by season, gravity talisman, Magic 8 Ball,
    Master Skulls in dungeons, IQ points (+% Intelligence).
  - **Damage taken by mob family:** zombie/undead, spider/arthropod, ender, wither, wolf/animal, skeleton,
    blaze/nether/infernal, sea creature, Leviathan followers (kuudra brood), and dojo belts (all damage).
  - **Damage dealt:** draconic (dragons), runeblade (axes vs woodland), demonlord gauntlet ×1.15, every 10th
    tarantula hit, wedding rings (double-damage chance), signal rings (crowded islands).
  - **Burststopper:** damage is multiplied by 0.95 or 0.9 when a hit would take at least 50% of your max HP.
  - **Kills and death:** piggy banks (save 100% or 75% of 20k+ death losses, then crack or break), scavenger
    coins per mob level, devour-ring heal, reaper orb stacks, the blood-god kill counter (strength per digit).
  - **Income and rewards:** emerald ring/artifact (5 and 20 coins a minute), coin talismans (random coins on
    public islands), bits talisman (10% double bits), experience artifact (+25% orbs), auto-recombobulator
    (1% of unique mob drops).
  - **Movement and protection:** feather talismans (fall damage), fire and lava talismans, intimidation (mobs at
    or below level N ignore you).
  - **Potions:** haste ring/artifact, night vision charm, balloon snake (jump boost).
  - **Equipment:** regen cloaks, the ender set and dragonfade multiplying their own stats on The End, set
    bonuses (mithril/titanium in the Dwarven Mines, gemstone gatherer in the Crystal Hollows), the tiered
    Angler / Arachne / Backwater bonuses (per piece, see wave 3), bone necklace (Defense per nearby enemy), lava
    shell (sets regen and mending to 0).
- **Personal compactor and deletor** (`/compactor`, `/deletor`, or right-click the item).
  - Slots: 1/3/7/12 for the 4000/5000/6000/7000.
  - You add an item by clicking it in your inventory.
  - The compactor turns any single-ingredient crafting recipe into its enchanted form, once a second.
  - The deletor cancels pickups and `ItemGain` events.
- **Enrichments.** Right-click one to pick a Legendary-or-better accessory in your bag to apply it to. It adds a
  stat through an `ItemStatContributor` (Source OTHER), so the lore and the stats agree. The swapper re-stats
  every enriched accessory in the bag at once.
- **Menus.** Accessory Bag → Thaumaturgy (Accessory Power breakdown, Powers, Stats Tuning + templates, Power
  Stones, Rift Prism) → Bag Upgrades → Equipment → Accessory Guide (missing/all, click → `/recipe <id>`).
  Every menu has a command.
- **NPC sell cap (no craft-and-sell coin printer).** A crafted accessory, equipment piece or power stone never
  sells to an NPC for more than `accessories.sell-cap-factor` (1.0) x what its CHEAPEST CRAFTING/FORGE recipe's
  inputs sell for. Our own items used as inputs count at their capped price (4 capped Emperor Talismans make the
  Ring's cap, not the reference 900); unknown ids and coins count 0, so an unknown can only lower a cap. Applied
  twice: the generator caps the shipped JSON against every other module's item data (it prints the list; 14
  items today, e.g. Delirium Necklace 52,000 -> 4,000, Scourge Cloak 57,000 -> 14,000, Scoville Belt 55,000 ->
  10,000, Emperor Ring/Artifact 900/8,100 -> 400/1,600, Respiration Talisman/Ring/Artifact -> 0 because Puff Crux
  has no NPC price, plus the gated Healing Ring, Runeblade Ring/Artifact, Haste Ring, Soulflow Pile, Vampire
  Dentist Relic), and `SellCap.apply` re-applies the rule in `postEnable` (and once more a tick later) against the
  prices the server really registered, so another module lowering an input price cannot reopen a loop. It only
  ever lowers. Unit tests: `SellCapTest` (6, including one that rebuilds every other module's prices from the
  shipped JSON and asserts no recipe of ours out-sells its inputs).
- **NPC.** "Thaumaturgist Ophira" stands at hub anchor `npc.thaumaturgist`, talks, then opens Thaumaturgy. It can
  be turned off with the dial `accessories.npc`.
- **Anti-dupe.**
  - Bag and equipment menus use `keepFreeSlotItems()` and save on every change and on close, with `saveNow`.
  - Every 30 s a sweep deletes an inventory copy of any unique item whose uuid is also stored in the bag or
    equipment, and logs it.
  - Unreadable stored bytes are never overwritten.

## Wave 3 (2026-10-04)
21 items that were lore-only now work, each as a typed effect written by the generator (so lore and behaviour come
from one spec), with no `extra.hook` left on them. All in `EffectEngine`; new `Fx` types in brackets.
- **Vaccine Talisman/Ring/Artifact** [`poison_resist`]: poison damage x0.9 / x0.75 / x0.5 (`EntityDamageEvent` POISON).
- **Magnetic Talisman** [`magnet`]: every 4 ticks, items you may pick up (no owner, or you) within 3 blocks drift to
  you. Other players' owned loot is never pulled.
- **Netherrack-Looking Sunshade** [`unseen`]: ghasts (entity GHAST or any mob id containing "ghast") cannot target you.
- **Blaze Belt / Vanquished Blaze Belt** [`reflect`]: 10% / 20% of fireball hits from blazes and ghasts (the hit
  carries core's `projectile` tag) are cancelled and the thrower takes the hit's raw damage next tick, with a flame
  trail and an action-bar line.
- **Gauntlet of Contagion** [`contagion`]: a kill bursts for 10% of the victim's max health (MAGIC, next tick) on
  every sky mob within 2 blocks; a chain of kills is a chain of ticks, never recursion.
- **Magma Necklace / Vanquished Magma Necklace** [`drop_boost`]: +10% / +20% MAGMA_CREAM from monster drops
  (fractional part rolled), given next tick as `LootSource.OTHER` so it can never boost itself.
- **Scoville Belt** [`nearby_def` with `mob: burning`]: +50 Defense per BURNING sky mob within 10 blocks, max 1,000.
- **Shadow Assassin Cloak (+starred)** [`tp_strike`]: a same-world teleport under 64 blocks (abilities, pearls) arms
  +10% on the next melee hit within 5 s; one hit spends it.
- **Dragonfuse Glove** [`weapon_buff`]: holding ASPECT_OF_THE_DRAGON gives +50 Strength (stat, re-statted on held
  item change by core) and +35 flat damage on weapon hits.
- **Implosion Belt** [`ability_boost`]: x1.25 on named abilities whose name contains implosion / explos / blast /
  wither impact (research 01: "Implosion Belt x1.25 explosion damage").
- **Thunderbolt Necklace** [`static`]: 1 charge per 30 s, max 2, +15% per charge on the next weapon hit. Skipped when
  the armour module's full Thunder set already discharged that hit (its `static_charge` tag, armour runs at 200).
- **Shriveled Bracelet** [`vs_stat`]: +5 Combat Wisdom and +10 Magic Find while your last hit (within 5 s) was on an
  arthropod. Stats re-compute when the target family changes and when it lapses.
- **Manticore Claw** [`pet_copy`]: 10% of the Health/Defense/Strength/CC/CD/Attack Speed/Ferocity/Intelligence/True
  Defense the "pets" stat provider gave on the last pass (`Sky.stats().breakdown`). A ThreadLocal guard stops the
  very first compute (nothing cached yet) recursing.
- **Vanquished Blaze Belt / Ghast Cloak / Magma Necklace / Glowstone Gauntlet** [`piece_bonus`]: the piece counts
  blazes / ghasts / magma cubes killed or GLOWSTONE_DUST gathers (crimson's `Gather` id) in its own item data
  (`extra.pieceCount`), and grants +1 Strength / Defense / Crit Damage / Intelligence per 1,000, max +10, through an
  `ItemStatContributor` (Source OTHER) so lore and stats agree. The lore shows the count and "Next upgrade: +n (x/1,000)";
  a chat line announces each step. Dials `accessories.piece-bonus-every` 1000, `accessories.piece-bonus-cap` 10 (TUNE).
- **Tiered set bonuses no longer pay a piece twice.** Angler (Depth Champion, Deepness Within), Arachne (Queen's
  Faithful) and Backwater (Swamp Soldier) are paid by the ARMOUR module per armour piece. Before, ours counted armour
  too, so a full armour set plus nothing else still triggered our bonus on top of armour's (Deepness Within: a second
  full +6 Health per Fishing level). Now `perPiece` sets count only equipment, per piece, up to the tier cap minus
  the armour pieces (`AccMath.tieredEquipPieces`): 4 armour + 4 equipment on an 8-tier = 4 here, on Deepness Within
  (4-tier, 1.5/level/piece like armour's dial) = 0 here. Lore now reads "Tiered Bonus: X (per piece worn, up to N)".
- Lore polish: "Grants +5 Health always." lost the "always".
- Self-test `accessories.effects` also checks `vs_stat`/`piece_bonus` have a stat and `weapon_buff`/`drop_boost`
  name a registered item.
- Verified: `bash tools/check.sh accessories` -> CHECK OK; **27/27 JUnit** (new `HookEffectsTest`, 7 tests: every
  number above read back from the shipped JSON, no hook left on any of the 21, ability matching, the tier split,

## Known risks
1. **Storage menus are the dupe surface.** The bag and equipment menus hold real items. Each change is written to
   member data at once and `saveNow` runs on close, but a hard crash between the vanilla inventory save and the
   profile save could lose or duplicate one item. The 30 s uuid sweep catches the duplicate case.
2. **Nothing has been exercised live.** Over RCON, start with `/sb selftest accessories`, then
   `/sb accessories fill <p>`, `/sb accessories info <p>` and `/sb stats <p>`. Damage-type matching depends on mob
   owners' defs. `accessories.recipes` will fail until materials, slayer and other modules register their
   ingredient ids; it prints which ones are missing.
3. **Numbers marked TUNE**, because research gives no exact value:
   - tuning value per point (research says "from memory")
   - bag pair cost curve and the redstone exchange cost
   - SkyBlock-level milestones standing in for the account upgrades
   - coin-talisman rates
   - gravity distance step
   - linear tiered set bonuses
   - Magic 8 Ball season roll (derived from uuid and season)

THE ARCHIVES

"They say the Precursors didn't die out. They say they ascended. We are just the dust they left behind."

// 01. CORE PROTOCOLS

Your directive is simple: Sustain the Star. The universe is freezing over, and you are the keeper of the last heat source. To do this, you must inject Hydrogen into the core to increase its Mass.

  • IGNITION: The star is cold. Clicking 'Inject Hydrogen' will ignite the core and begin the cycle.
  • CONSUMPTION: Once ignited, the star consumes Hydrogen automatically based on its Mass. Larger stars are hungrier.
  • OUTPUT: Burning Hydrogen produces Energy (J). Use this to automate your task.

// 02. THE GREAT FILTER

All stars eventually die. As Mass increases, the core pressure allows for the fusion of heavier elements.

  • THRESHOLD: Once Mass exceeds the safety threshold, IRON begins to form.
  • TOXICITY: Iron is the ash of stars. It cannot be fused. It reduces your Energy generation efficiency.
  • COLLAPSE: If Iron Toxicity reaches 100%, the core destabilizes. Supernova is inevitable.

// 03. ADVANCED SYSTEMS

LABORATORY: Spend Energy to research ancient technologies. Unlocks alien contact and star mapping.

CONSTELLATIONS: Connect distant stars using Insight to gain permanent passive buffs.

PRESTIGE: When the star collapses, you gather Stardust. This allows you to rewrite the laws of physics for the next universe.

"Do not fear the end. The end is just a reset button."

# 🧟 Zombies Incremental: Protocol Zero

An incremental/idle clicker game where you fight zombies across sectors, collect resources, and progress through multiple prestige layers. Features deep progression systems, equippable anomalies, passive artifacts, and special mechanics like "Subject Zero" and the Singularity.

## 🎮 Quick Start

Simply open `Zombies Take 2.html` in your web browser. No installation, dependencies, or build process required!

**Play it now:** Open the HTML file in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).

## ✨ Features

- **5 Prestige Layers**: Mutagens → Neural Links → Stardust → Dark Matter → Singularity
- **Click & Idle Gameplay**: Active clicking or passive DPS progression
- **10 Equippable Anomalies**: Powerful items with unique effects (unlocked post-Singularity)
- **Artifact Collection**: Passive bonuses from rare drops (7 rarity tiers)
- **Special Encounters**: Meet Subject Zero in Sector 5
- **Hyper Mode**: Challenge mode with 10× HP enemies
- **Neural Shop**: Advanced upgrades with Neural Links currency
- **Cosmic Upgrades**: Warp Drive and Hyper-Mapping systems
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Exponential enemy scaling across sectors

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Click enemies** to deal damage and collect **Biomass**
2. **Purchase upgrades** to increase your click damage and idle DPS
3. **Progress through sectors** by killing 10 normal enemies + 1 boss
4. **Reach Sector 20+** to unlock your first prestige: **Mutagens**
5. **Prestige** to gain permanent bonuses and unlock new currencies
6. **Progress through 5 prestige layers** to reach the **Singularity**
7. **Collect Anomalies and Artifacts** (unlocked after first Singularity)

### Prestige Progression

1. **Mutagens (L1)**: +10% damage per Mutagen | Unlock at Sector 20+
2. **Neural Links (L2)**: Currency for Neural Shop | Unlock at 10+ Mutagens
3. **Stardust (L3)**: +1% crit chance per Stardust | Unlock at 5+ Neural Links
4. **Dark Matter (L4)**: ×100 damage per Dark Matter | Unlock at 50+ Stardust
5. **Singularity (L5)**: 5^Universe damage multiplier | Unlock at 100+ Dark Matter

Each prestige layer requires consuming the previous layer's currency, creating a challenging progression path.

## 🎨 Game Mechanics

### Combat System
- **Click Damage**: Direct damage from clicking enemies
- **Idle DPS**: Automatic damage per second (10 ticks/sec)
- **Critical Hits**: Chance-based with multiplier bonuses
- **Boss Fights**: 30-second timer to defeat or boss retreats

### Special Systems
- **Subject Zero**: Special encounter in Sector 5 with unique dialogue
- **Hyper Mode**: Toggle for 10× HP enemies (unlocked with 20 Stardust)
- **Auto Advance**: Automatically engage bosses when ready
- **Warp Drive**: Chance to skip stages (Cosmic Upgrade)

### Post-Singularity Features
- **Anomalies**: Equip 3 powerful items from 10 available (0.1% drop rate)
- **Artifacts**: Collect passive bonuses (50% drop from bosses, weighted by rarity)

## 🎯 Tips for New Players

1. **Focus on Idle Upgrades**: They provide consistent DPS even when not clicking
2. **Wait for Sector 40+ before first prestige**: You'll get 2 Mutagens instead of 1
3. **Neural Shop Priority**: Auto-clicker (Synaptic Drone) is very helpful
4. **Save Stardust for Hyper-Mapping**: Unlocks Hyper Mode at 20 Stardust
5. **Don't worry about prestige timing**: The game is designed for multiple runs
6. **Subject Zero is a story element**: Doesn't provide special rewards, just unique dialogue

## 📝 Game Progression Tips

- **Early Game (Sectors 1-20)**: Focus on upgrading both click and idle damage evenly
- **Mid Game (Sectors 20-40)**: First prestige around Sector 40 for better gains
- **Neural Era**: Prioritize auto-clicker, then discount, then overclock
- **Stardust Era**: Save for Hyper-Mapping (20) before spending on Warp Drive
- **Dark Matter Era**: This is where the game gets interesting - massive damage multipliers
- **Singularity Era**: Anomalies and Artifacts create exponential scaling opportunities

## 🐛 Known Limitations

- Game state is not saved between sessions (by design)
- No offline progression
- No export/import save functionality
